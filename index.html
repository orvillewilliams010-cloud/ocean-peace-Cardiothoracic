Sternal-Sparing Precision

The Future of Heart Surgery: Robotic Port-Access

Traditional cardiac surgery requires dividing the sternum (breastbone), which takes 8 to 12 weeks to heal. Robotic and port-access surgery operates through tiny 2-4 cm lateral rib-space incisions without breaking a single bone.

Robotic Port-Access
  • ✓ No sternal division (Zero bone break)
  • ✓ 2 to 3 days hospital stay
  • ✓ Full driving clearance in 10-14 days
  • ✓ Minimal scar line (hidden under arm)
Traditional Open Sternotomy
  • ✕ Full 8-inch sternal bone incision
  • ✕ 5 to 7 days hospital stay
  • ✕ 8 to 12 weeks strict lifting limits
  • ✕ High postoperative pain score

Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)

On-pump and Beating-Heart (Off-Pump) surgical revascularization for multi-vessel coronary artery disease utilizing autologous arterial conduits.

3 - 5 Hours

Advanced Valve Repair & Reconstruction

State-of-the-art repair and bioprosthetic replacement of Aortic, Mitral, and Tricuspid valve disease prioritizing native leaflet preservation.

2.5 - 4 Hours

Minimally Invasive & Robotic Surgery

Port-access surgery without sternal division. Minimizes postoperative recovery time, incision scarring, and hospital stay.

3 - 4 Hours

VATS & Robotic Thoracic Lobectomy

Video-assisted thoracoscopic and robotic lung surgery for pulmonary nodules, lobar resections, and mediastinal tumor clearance.

2 - 3 Hours

Aortic Root & Aneurysm Reconstruction

High-acuity surgical repair of ascending aortic aneurysms, valve-sparing root reconstructions (David procedure), and acute dissections.
